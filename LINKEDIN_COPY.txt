PROJECT NAME
Portfolio Risk Lab | Python

DESCRIPTION
Built a reproducible portfolio optimisation and out-of-sample risk analysis pipeline in Python. The baseline samples 20,000 long-only allocations across five synthetic assets, computes a 40-point efficient frontier and compares equal-weight, minimum-volatility and maximum-Sharpe portfolios on a chronologically separated holdout. Extended the analysis across 250 independent synthetic market paths and three volatility regimes to measure holdout Sharpe distributions, strategy win rates, train-to-holdout estimation gaps and allocation-weight stability. Validated the implementation with 22 automated tests and reproducible reports.
