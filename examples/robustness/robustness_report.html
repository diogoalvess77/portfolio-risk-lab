Interpretation

  • Repeated paths reduce dependence on one particular random seed.
  • Training Sharpe is an estimate; the train-vs-holdout plot makes estimation error visible.
  • Win rates are descriptive within this synthetic design, not claims of real-world superiority.
  • Weight dispersion highlights how optimisation can react strongly to noisy sample estimates.
